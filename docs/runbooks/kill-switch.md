# Runbook: material-action proposal kill switch

Trigger when approval integrity, identity, policy grounding, model safety or customer outcome controls are uncertain.

1. Incident commander records reason, scope and start time.
2. Disable creation of material proposals; retain read-only case evidence and manual procedures.
3. Confirm API metrics show zero new material proposals and preserve audit ingestion.
4. Notify investigation operations, service owner, model risk and customer support with plain-language impact.
5. Reconcile proposals created during the incident window against the decision ledger.
6. Identify and test the correction in shadow mode. Do not reuse previous approvals.
7. Two named owners authorise restoration. Ramp gradually and monitor conflicts, volumes and complaints.
8. Publish a blameless review with control actions and due dates.
